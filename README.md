# Systematic Trading in Commodity Markets

Backtesting momentum, trend following and mean reversion on gold, crude oil, natural gas and copper ETFs (2012–2026), with robustness tests for trading costs, time periods and parameter settings.

The full analysis is in [`01_commodity_systematic_research.ipynb`](01_commodity_systematic_research.ipynb).

**Main finding:** most strategies looked better than Buy & Hold at first, but few advantages survived the robustness tests. The most consistent result was smaller drawdowns across all four markets.
